Block a number

Takes effect on the next call or text from that number. There is nothing to switch on afterwards.

Who to block
A single number, several at once, everyone from an area or country, or callers who hide their number.
Number
Type it the way it was shown to you. Paste several separated by commas or new lines. A number without a country code is read as US.
Prefix
The country code and, usually, the area code: +1 415 stops every San Francisco number, +44 every UK number.
What to stop
Blocking calls blocks faxes too — they arrive over the same line.
What the caller gets
The caller hears the out-of-service tones and the call ends. Nothing reaches you.
Whose numbers
You can block a caller for the whole company, or for any of its numbers.
Reason
Why — so the list still makes sense in six months.
Label and note
A name for the list, and anything worth remembering.
Emergency numbers, short codes and your own numbers can’t be blocked. Blocking one of your numbers stops that caller reaching you; blocking for the company stops them reaching any number the company owns, and needs an admin. Texts from a blocked sender are never delivered, whatever you’ve set the caller to hear.
